editorsTake: "GMX V2 Perps holds approximately $418M in TVL, operating primarily on Arbitrum with expansion to other chains. GMX pioneered the GLP model for perpetual DEXs - where liquidity providers deposit into a multi-asset pool that serves as counterparty to all trades - and V2 refines this approach with isolated pools and improved capital efficiency.

The platform offers perpetual contracts with up to 100x leverage on major pairs. V2 introduced GM tokens (replacing GLP), allowing liquidity provision to specific markets rather than a general pool. This means LPs can choose exposure - providing liquidity only to BTC-USD rather than a basket of assets. Current GM pool APYs range from 15-40% depending on the market and trading activity.

GMX's execution model uses Chainlink oracles for pricing, eliminating slippage on the oracle price but introducing oracle dependency. This zero-slippage model is particularly attractive for large trades that would face significant impact on order book DEXs. A $1M position can be opened at the oracle price, which is impossible on most venues.

The tradeoffs involve the oracle model's limitations. While zero slippage sounds ideal, it can work against traders during rapid price movements - the oracle price may lag spot market prices, creating temporary arbitrage opportunities that LPs benefit from. Additionally, GM token LPs face impermanent loss-like risk if traders consistently profit on a particular market.

GMX's fee structure is transparent but not cheap. Opening and closing positions incurs a 0.05-0.1% fee (depending on whether you're increasing or decreasing open interest), plus borrowing fees that accumulate over time. For active traders making frequent position adjustments, these fees compound meaningfully."
