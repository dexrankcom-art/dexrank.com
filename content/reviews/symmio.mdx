The infrastructure approach means SYMMIO's TVL represents liquidity backing multiple front-end platforms. IntentX, Thena, and others use SYMMIO's contracts and solver network. This B2B model differs from consumer-facing perpetual DEXs.

Intent-based settlement through SYMMIO enables competitive execution as solvers bid to fill user orders. This can improve pricing compared to fixed-spread AMMs, especially for larger orders where solver competition becomes meaningful.

For end users, SYMMIO matters indirectly. You likely won't interact with SYMMIO directly but rather through platforms built on it. The protocol's health affects these front-ends' ability to offer competitive execution and liquidity."
