The limitation is TON ecosystem size relative to established chains. While TON has grown significantly, its DeFi ecosystem remains smaller than Ethereum, Solana, or Arbitrum. Storm Trade leads perpetuals on TON but competes against much larger platforms on other chains. Traders outside TON face onboarding friction to access Storm."
