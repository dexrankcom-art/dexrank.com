Funding rates on perpetual exchanges fluctuate based on market sentiment and position imbalances. These rates can swing from strongly positive (longs paying shorts) to strongly negative during market shifts. Boros enables trading these movements directly, creating hedging opportunities for active perpetual traders.

The use case appeals primarily to sophisticated traders running funding rate arbitrage strategies or large position holders who want to hedge their funding exposure. A trader paying significant funding on a long position elsewhere could take an offsetting position on Boros to neutralize that cost.

The narrow focus limits Boros's addressable market. Most retail traders don't think about funding rate exposure as a tradeable asset. The protocol serves a real need but for a specialized audience. TVL reflects this niche positioning."
