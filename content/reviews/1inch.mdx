editorsTake: "1inch holds approximately $4M TVL in its derivatives offerings, though the protocol is primarily known as the leading DEX aggregator for spot trading. The aggregator has processed billions in cumulative spot volume by routing trades across multiple DEXs.

The spot aggregation functionality finds optimal routing across Uniswap, SushiSwap, Curve, and dozens of other DEXs. For large spot trades, this routing often achieves better execution than any single venue. The Fusion mode enables gasless swaps through a resolver network.

1inch's derivatives products are newer additions to the ecosystem. The Limit Order Protocol enables decentralized limit orders, while other derivative features continue development. These offerings don't yet match the spot aggregator's dominance.

The consideration is that 1inch excels at spot aggregation but isn't a dedicated perpetual DEX. Users seeking perpetual trading should use specialized platforms. For spot trading, particularly larger orders benefiting from routing, 1inch remains the standard."
