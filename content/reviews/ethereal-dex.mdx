editorsTake: "Ethereal DEX operates with approximately $71M in TVL, positioned as a perpetual trading platform focused on user experience and capital efficiency. The platform has gained attention for its clean interface and focus on making leveraged trading accessible without sacrificing functionality.

The platform offers perpetual contracts on major pairs with leverage up to 50x. Ethereal uses an oracle-based pricing model combined with a liquidity vault that serves as counterparty to trades. What distinguishes it from similar models is the emphasis on UX - the trading interface prioritizes clarity over feature density, making it more approachable for traders coming from centralized exchanges.

Ethereal's liquidity vault operates on a familiar model where depositors earn from trading fees and trader losses while bearing the risk of trader profits. Current vault APYs have ranged from 15-35% depending on trading activity and market conditions. The vault accepts multiple stablecoins, providing some flexibility for depositors.

One notable feature is Ethereal's approach to liquidation protection. The platform offers optional liquidation buffers that traders can purchase, providing additional cushion before forced closure. This adds a layer of risk management that some competitors lack, though it comes at a cost that reduces effective returns.

The tradeoffs involve the platform's relative size and maturity. At $71M TVL, Ethereal is smaller than leading venues, which can translate to less deep liquidity for larger positions. The team has been building steadily, but the operational track record is shorter than established protocols. For traders moving size or seeking battle-tested infrastructure, this is worth considering."
