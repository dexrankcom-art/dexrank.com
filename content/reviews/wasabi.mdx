editorsTake: "Wasabi differentiates itself in the perpetuals space by focusing on options and structured products alongside standard perpetual contracts, with approximately $29M TVL. This positions Wasabi as more of a derivatives suite than a pure perpetuals platform.

The options functionality allows traders to hedge positions or speculate with defined risk - you know your maximum loss upfront when buying options. Combined with perpetuals, this creates sophisticated trading possibilities like protective puts on leveraged longs. Such strategies typically require multiple platforms; Wasabi consolidates them.

Wasabi's execution uses an intent-based system where market makers fill orders. This can provide better pricing than pure AMM models but relies on active market maker participation. During low activity periods or extreme volatility, fill quality may vary.

The consideration with Wasabi is complexity. Options trading requires more knowledge than spot or basic perpetuals. Users who don't understand options pricing may find portions of the platform confusing or accidentally enter unfavorable trades. The protocol suits traders already familiar with derivatives strategies."
