editorsTake: "Mooncake operates with approximately $3M TVL, offering perpetual trading with a focus on community-driven development and governance. The platform emphasizes decentralization in both trading and protocol direction.

The governance focus means token holders actively influence protocol parameters, fee structures, and development priorities. This appeals to users who want voice in the platforms they use rather than accepting top-down decisions from development teams.

Mooncake's perpetual trading implements familiar mechanics - leverage, funding rates, liquidity pools. The differentiation comes from governance involvement rather than technical innovation. Trading experience is adequate for medium-sized positions.

The tradeoff is that decentralized governance can slow development compared to teams with clear leadership. Major changes require community consensus, which takes time. Users who value governance participation accept this pace; those prioritizing rapid feature development may prefer centralized alternatives."
