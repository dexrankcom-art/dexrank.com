editorsTake: "Aevo operates with approximately $25M TVL, combining perpetual futures with options trading on its own rollup. The platform emerged from Ribbon Finance's options expertise and expanded into perpetuals to offer a comprehensive derivatives suite.

The platform runs on Aevo L2, an Optimism-based rollup dedicated to derivatives trading. This gives Aevo control over the execution environment while maintaining Ethereum security for settlements. Trading feels responsive with sub-second order confirmations and gas costs subsidized by the protocol.

Aevo's options background influences its perpetual design. The platform offers pre-launch futures for tokens before they trade on spot markets - a popular feature for speculation on upcoming launches. These pre-launch markets have seen significant volume around major token generation events.

The consideration is Aevo's competitive position against larger perpetual DEXs. While the options+perpetuals combination is valuable, pure perpetual traders may find better liquidity on focused platforms. Aevo works best for traders who utilize both product types or specifically want pre-launch futures access."
