Polynomial's distinguishing feature is its vault system that automates trading strategies. Rather than manually managing positions, users can deposit into vaults that execute predefined strategies - covered calls, put selling, or delta-neutral approaches. This systematizes trading for passive participants.

The manual trading interface provides standard perpetual functionality for active traders. Both approaches share underlying liquidity, creating flexibility between active and passive modes. The Optimism deployment keeps gas costs manageable.

The complexity is that vault strategies require trust in the automated systems. Users must understand what each vault does and accept that algorithmic execution may not match manual trading performance during unusual market conditions."
