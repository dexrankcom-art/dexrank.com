editorsTake: "Reya Perps operates with approximately $27M TVL on its own purpose-built L2 network, representing one of the few perpetual protocols that went the app-chain route. This architectural choice prioritizes trading performance over ecosystem network effects.

The dedicated L2 allows Reya to optimize every aspect of the chain for derivatives trading - block times, gas costs, and throughput are all tuned for the trading use case. This contrasts with building on general-purpose L2s where perpetual DEXs compete for blockspace with DeFi, NFTs, and other applications.

Reya's passive liquidity model lets LPs deposit without active management, with the protocol handling market-making algorithmically. Historical returns for LPs have been competitive, though like all perpetual DEX liquidity provision, periods of sustained directional movement can produce drawdowns.

The tradeoff with app-chains is ecosystem isolation. Assets must bridge to Reya's network, introducing friction and bridge risk. Users comfortable with the bridging process get optimized trading; those preferring to stay on familiar chains like Arbitrum or Solana may find general-purpose perpetual DEXs more convenient."
