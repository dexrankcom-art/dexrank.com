editorsTake: "IntentX operates with approximately $6M TVL, pioneering intent-based perpetual trading where users express desired outcomes and market makers compete to fill them. This model differs from traditional order books or AMMs.

Intent-based execution means traders submit what they want (e.g., long 10 ETH at market price) and solvers compete to provide the best execution. This can achieve better pricing than single-venue execution, especially for larger orders that would otherwise face slippage.

IntentX builds on the SYMMIO framework, which standardizes intent-based derivatives across multiple front-ends. This modular approach means IntentX competes on user experience while sharing underlying infrastructure with other SYMMIO implementations.

The complexity is that intent-based systems behave differently than traditional exchanges. Fill times may vary based on solver competition, and exotic orders might not attract solver interest. Users accustomed to instant limit order fills may find the model unfamiliar."
