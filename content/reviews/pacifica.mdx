Pacifica differentiates through its risk management approach. Whether these mechanisms prove sufficient during extreme volatility remains to be fully tested.

The main consideration for traders is liquidity depth. At $47M TVL, Pacifica can handle moderate-sized positions but larger traders may experience slippage on substantial orders. The platform works best for position sizes under $500K where its execution remains competitive."
