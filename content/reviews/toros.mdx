editorsTake: "Toros holds approximately $17M TVL, specializing in automated leveraged products rather than traditional perpetual trading. The platform creates tokenized leverage positions - users can hold a 2x or 3x leveraged ETH position as a single token without managing collateral.

This approach differs fundamentally from perpetual trading. Instead of opening positions with margin, traders simply buy leveraged tokens that automatically rebalance to maintain target leverage. No liquidation risk in the traditional sense - the tokens can decline toward zero but won't wipe out more than invested.

Toros builds on Synthetix V3 infrastructure, benefiting from its oracle network and synthetic asset capabilities. The integration means Toros tokens track their targets accurately while maintaining on-chain composability - these tokens work in other DeFi protocols.

The consideration is that leveraged tokens suffer from volatility decay over time. Holding a 3x BTC token during sideways, choppy markets erodes value even if BTC returns to its starting price. These products suit directional conviction trades, not long-term holds."
pros:

  - "No active position management required"
