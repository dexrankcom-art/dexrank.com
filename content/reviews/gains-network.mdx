editorsTake: "Gains Network (gTrade) pioneered synthetic perpetuals on Polygon with approximately $26M TVL remaining, though the protocol has expanded to Arbitrum. At its peak during 2022-2023, Gains processed substantial volume and introduced innovations that competitors later adopted.

The synthetic model means positions don't require actual underlying assets - prices come from oracles and profits/losses settle in DAI or the platform's native collateral. This allows Gains to offer pairs beyond standard crypto, including forex and commodities, with consistent 24/7 trading regardless of traditional market hours.

Gains' claim to fame was high leverage availability - up to 150x on crypto pairs and 1000x on forex. While such leverage enables capital-efficient speculation, it also means small price movements cause liquidations. The platform's liquidation engine has processed countless positions during volatility events.

The current consideration is that Gains faces intense competition from newer protocols with larger liquidity and more active development. The protocol continues operating but has lost market share to Jupiter Perps on Solana, GMX on Arbitrum, and Hyperliquid on its own chain. Remaining users appreciate the familiar interface and synthetic asset variety."
