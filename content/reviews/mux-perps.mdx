editorsTake: "MUX Perps operates with approximately $13M TVL as part of the MUX Protocol ecosystem, which combines perpetual trading with a derivatives aggregator. The platform routes orders across multiple perpetual DEXs to find optimal execution.

The aggregation model differentiates MUX from single-venue perpetual DEXs. When opening a position, MUX can split execution across GMX, Gains, and other protocols to achieve better average pricing than any single source. This appeals to larger traders who would otherwise face slippage on concentrated orders.

MUX maintains its own perpetual trading venue while also aggregating others. Users can choose direct MUX execution or aggregated routing depending on position size and market conditions. The MUXLP token lets LPs earn from both MUX's native trading and aggregation fees.

The complexity is that aggregation introduces dependencies on multiple protocols. If one source protocol has issues, MUX routing must adapt. The system generally handles this well, but adds technical surface area compared to single-protocol alternatives."
