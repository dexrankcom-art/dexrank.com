editorsTake: "Extended operates with approximately $203M in TVL, positioning itself as a perpetual DEX focused on novel margin mechanisms and cross-collateralization. The platform has gained attention for its approach to capital efficiency, allowing traders to use a wider range of assets as collateral for perpetual positions.

The platform's core innovation is its flexible collateral system. Unlike most perpetual DEXs that require specific stablecoins or the asset being traded as margin, Extended accepts multiple collateral types that can be cross-margined across positions. This means traders can post yield-bearing assets as collateral while maintaining leveraged positions, effectively earning yield on their margin.

Extended offers perpetual contracts on major pairs with competitive leverage options. The platform uses a hybrid liquidity model combining aspects of both order books and AMM-style pools, attempting to capture the benefits of both - tighter spreads from market makers and consistent liquidity from pools.

The tradeoff with cross-collateral systems is increased complexity in risk management. When collateral assets are volatile, position health can change due to collateral price movements independent of the traded asset. This creates additional variables traders must monitor, and liquidation conditions become more complex to reason about.

The platform is relatively newer compared to established names like GMX or dYdX, which means less operational history to assess. The team has conducted audits and implemented standard security practices, but the cross-collateral mechanics introduce novel risk vectors that haven't been stress-tested across multiple market cycles. For yield-focused strategies using the collateral flexibility, this novelty is worth considering."
