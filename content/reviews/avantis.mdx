title: Avantis Review
tier: 1
editorsTake: "Avantis operates with approximately $107M in TVL, positioning itself as a perpetual DEX on Base (Coinbase's L2) with a focus on accessible leverage trading. The platform has grown rapidly alongside Base's ecosystem expansion, benefiting from the chain's low fees and Coinbase's distribution advantages.

The platform uses a GMX-style liquidity model where LPs deposit into a vault that serves as counterparty to all trades. Avantis differentiates through its integration of real-world assets (RWA) and forex pairs alongside crypto perpetuals - traders can access leverage on EUR/USD, gold, and other traditional markets directly on-chain. This cross-asset approach is relatively rare among perpetual DEXs.

Leverage options go up to 100x on major pairs, with lower limits on more exotic markets. The oracle system uses a combination of Chainlink and Pyth for pricing, providing redundancy against single-oracle failures. Fees are competitive with the broader perpetual DEX market, typically 0.06-0.08% to open or close positions.

The Base ecosystem offers meaningful advantages for Avantis users. Transaction costs are minimal (often under $0.10), and Coinbase's fiat onramps create an accessible path for newer DeFi users. The chain's focus on consumer applications means Avantis benefits from ecosystem-wide user acquisition efforts.

The tradeoffs center on Base's relative youth as a chain and Avantis's position as a newer protocol. While audited and operational, the platform hasn't experienced the same duration of battle-testing as GMX or dYdX. Additionally, while RWA and forex markets are interesting additions, their liquidity is thinner than crypto pairs, which can mean worse execution on larger positions in those markets."
pros:
