editorsTake: "Kinetiq Markets operates with approximately $23M TVL as a perpetual trading venue on Hyperliquid's ecosystem. Building on the Hyperliquid L1, Kinetiq inherits the chain's sub-second finality and on-chain order book infrastructure.

The platform focuses on providing perpetual trading with tight integration to Hyperliquid's broader DeFi ecosystem. This means traders can move between Kinetiq and other Hyperliquid applications without bridging delays. The unified experience appeals to users already committed to the Hyperliquid ecosystem.

Kinetiq's execution benefits from Hyperliquid's technical foundation - the same architecture that enables Hyperliquid's flagship exchange powers Kinetiq's matching. This means professional-grade performance without the infrastructure investment of running a dedicated chain.

The consideration is ecosystem lock-in. Kinetiq works best for traders already using Hyperliquid products. Those on Arbitrum, Solana, or other chains face bridging friction to access Kinetiq. The platform succeeds within its ecosystem but isn't designed for cross-chain traders."
