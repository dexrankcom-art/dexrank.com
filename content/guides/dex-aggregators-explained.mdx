## What is a DEX Aggregator?

A **DEX aggregator** searches multiple decentralized exchanges simultaneously to find the best price for your trade. Instead of manually comparing rates across Uniswap, SushiSwap, and Curve, the aggregator does it automatically.

For large trades, aggregators can split your order across multiple DEXs and routes to minimize price impact.

## How Aggregators Work

### Price Discovery

When you request a quote:
1. Aggregator queries all supported DEXs
2. Calculates prices including gas costs
3. Simulates complex routes (A to B to C)
4. Returns the best option

This happens in seconds and considers factors you would need hours to manually calculate.

### Route Splitting

For a $100,000 ETH to USDC swap:
- 40% through Uniswap v3 0.05% pool
- 30% through Curve tricrypto
- 20% through Balancer
- 10% through SushiSwap

Splitting reduces price impact at each venue, resulting in more tokens received.

### Gas Optimization

Complex routes cost more gas. Aggregators balance:
- Better prices from multi-hop routes
- Higher gas from more contract calls
- Net benefit after all costs

Sometimes the cheapest route is a simple direct swap. Aggregators calculate the full picture.

## Major Aggregators

### Jupiter (Solana)

The dominant aggregator on Solana with over 90% market share.

**Features:**
- Routes through all major Solana DEXs
- Built-in limit orders
- DCA (dollar-cost averaging) automation
- Perp trading integration

**Why popular:** Solana users rarely bypass Jupiter because it consistently finds better prices than individual DEXs.

### 1inch (EVM Chains)

The largest EVM aggregator, supporting Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, and more.

**Features:**
- Pathfinder algorithm for optimal routes
- Fusion mode for gasless trades
- Limit orders
- Portfolio tracking

**Why popular:** Cross-chain support and advanced routing across fragmented EVM liquidity.

### Paraswap (EVM Chains)

Competitive alternative to 1inch with strong institutional adoption.

**Features:**
- MultiPath routing algorithm
- Gas token support
- API access for integrations

### CowSwap (Ethereum)

Uses batch auctions instead of on-chain routing.

**Features:**
- MEV protection by design
- Gasless trading (fees in sell token)
- Peer-to-peer matching when possible

**Why popular:** Strong MEV protection through batch auction design.

## When to Use Aggregators

### Use Aggregators When:

**Large trades ($1,000+):** Savings from better routing exceed any aggregator fees.

**Illiquid tokens:** Aggregators find liquidity across obscure pools you might miss.

**Multi-hop swaps:** Converting Token A to Token C via Token B is complex manually.

**You want MEV protection:** Some aggregators route through protected channels.

### Use Direct DEX When:

**Very small trades:** Aggregator overhead may not provide value on $50 swaps.

**Simple pairs with deep liquidity:** ETH/USDC on Uniswap is hard to beat.

**You need specific pools:** LP position management requires direct DEX access.

**Gas-sensitive situations:** Aggregator routes sometimes use more gas.

## Aggregator Economics

### How They Make Money

Most aggregators earn through:
- Small spread between quoted and executed price
- Transaction priority (MEV capture shared with users)
- Token incentives (governance tokens)
- Premium features (API access, advanced orders)

### User Costs

Aggregators typically do not charge explicit fees. Their revenue comes from execution optimization, not user charges.

However, always compare:
1. Net tokens received (after all costs)
2. Gas costs of the proposed route
3. What you would get from direct DEX trading

## Evaluating Aggregator Quotes

A quote from an aggregator should show:
- Input amount
- Expected output amount
- Price impact
- Gas estimate
- Route details

Compare this to a direct DEX quote for the same trade. The difference is your savings (or cost).

### Example Comparison

**$10,000 USDC to ETH swap:**

| Route | Output | Gas | Net |
|-------|--------|-----|-----|
| 1inch | 4.182 ETH | $2.50 | 4.182 ETH - $2.50 |
| Uniswap direct | 4.175 ETH | $1.80 | 4.175 ETH - $1.80 |

1inch provides 0.007 more ETH but costs $0.70 more gas. At $2,400/ETH, the extra ETH is worth $16.80. Net benefit: $16.10 better through 1inch.

## Advanced Features

### Limit Orders

Place orders that execute when price reaches your target. Not all DEXs support this natively, but aggregators add the functionality.

### DCA Automation

Schedule regular purchases over time. Jupiter's DCA feature buys fixed amounts at intervals.

### MEV Protection

CowSwap and 1inch Fusion batch orders and protect against sandwich attacks. Your trade goes through private channels rather than public mempool.

## Aggregator Limitations

### Not Always Best

For standard pairs with deep liquidity, direct trading can match or beat aggregators while using less gas.

### Smart Contract Risk

Aggregators add another contract layer. While major aggregators are well-audited, they represent additional attack surface.

### Quote Accuracy

Market conditions change between quote and execution. High volatility can cause quotes to miss estimates.

## Practical Tips

1. **Compare before large trades:** Check aggregator versus direct DEX for trades over $5,000.

2. **Watch gas estimates:** Complex routes can cost 2-3x the gas of direct swaps.

3. **Use native chain aggregators:** Jupiter for Solana, 1inch for EVM chains.

4. **Enable MEV protection:** CowSwap on Ethereum, private transaction modes elsewhere.

5. **Check slippage settings:** Aggregators respect your slippage tolerance but may fail more complex routes.

## Summary

DEX aggregators find optimal prices by searching multiple exchanges and constructing complex routes. They provide the most value for large trades and illiquid tokens.

For routine trading, aggregators save time and often money. For very small trades on liquid pairs, direct DEX access may be simpler without much price difference.
