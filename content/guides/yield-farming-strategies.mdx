## What is Yield Farming?

Yield farming means deploying capital across DeFi protocols to earn returns. For DEX users, this typically involves providing liquidity to earn trading fees plus additional token rewards.

Returns come from multiple sources: swap fees, governance token emissions, and sometimes staking rewards on LP positions.

## Yield Sources on DEXs

### Trading Fees

The baseline return for liquidity providers. Fees range from 0.01% to 1% per swap, distributed proportionally to LPs.

**Calculation:** APY = (Daily Volume * Fee Rate * 365) / TVL

A $10M pool with $5M daily volume and 0.3% fees:
- Daily fees: $15,000
- Annual fees: $5.475M
- Fee APY: 54.75%

Real fee APYs vary widely based on volume and liquidity.

### Token Incentives

Protocols distribute governance tokens to attract liquidity. These "farms" pay tokens to LP stakers beyond trading fees.

**Example:** Deposit LP tokens in Sushi farm, earn SUSHI rewards.

Incentive APYs can reach 100%+ but decline as:
- More LPs compete for rewards
- Token price drops
- Emission schedules decrease

### Voting Incentives

Some protocols (Curve, Balancer) let vote-locked token holders direct rewards. Projects bribe voters to send emissions their way.

**Flywheel:** Provide liquidity > Earn governance tokens > Lock tokens > Vote for your pool > Earn more rewards.

## Evaluating Farm Opportunities

### APY vs. APR

**APR:** Annual percentage rate, simple interest
**APY:** Annual percentage yield, compound interest

A 100% APR compounded daily = 171% APY. Always check which metric is displayed.

### Sustainable vs. Unsustainable Yields

**Sustainable:** Fee-based returns proportional to genuine trading activity.

**Unsustainable:** Token emissions that dilute holders. 500% APY paid in inflationary tokens often becomes negative real return.

### Risk Assessment

High yields compensate for high risks:
- Smart contract risk
- Impermanent loss
- Token price collapse
- Rug pull potential

A 20% yield on a battle-tested protocol may be better than 200% on an unknown fork.

## LP Farming Strategies

### Single Pool Focus

Concentrate capital in one high-conviction opportunity.

**Pros:** Simple, maximizes specific exposure
**Cons:** Concentrated risk

### Diversified LP Portfolio

Spread across multiple pools and protocols.

**Example allocation:**
- 30% Curve stablecoins
- 25% Uniswap v3 ETH/USDC
- 25% Balancer weighted pools
- 20% Newer farms for higher yields

**Pros:** Risk distribution
**Cons:** Higher gas costs, more management

### Stablecoin Farming

Focus on stable pairs (USDC/USDT, FRAX/USDC) to avoid impermanent loss.

**Returns:** Lower (5-20% typically) but consistent
**Risk:** Smart contract, stablecoin depeg

Good for conservative capital deployment.

### Delta Neutral Strategies

Combine LP positions with hedges to neutralize directional exposure.

**Example:**
1. Provide ETH/USDC liquidity
2. Short ETH perps equal to LP ETH exposure
3. Collect fees while hedged against ETH price moves

**Complexity:** Requires active management and perp access
**Returns:** Fee income minus funding costs

## Compounding and Harvesting

### Manual Compounding

1. Claim rewards periodically
2. Swap reward tokens to LP tokens
3. Reinvest in the farm

Gas costs eat into returns. Calculate break-even frequency.

### Auto-Compounders

Protocols like Yearn, Beefy, and Convex automate compounding:
- Pool user deposits
- Harvest rewards at optimal intervals
- Compound efficiently with batched transactions
- Charge management fee (typically 2-10%)

**Tradeoff:** Additional smart contract risk, fees reduce net yield.

### Harvest Timing

Claim rewards when:
- Gas is low (weekends, off-hours)
- Reward token price is high
- You plan to sell or compound immediately

Unclaimed rewards are exposed to smart contract risk.

## Protocol-Specific Strategies

### Curve Finance

**CRV emissions:** Base rewards for LPs
**Boost:** Lock CRV as veCRV to boost rewards up to 2.5x
**Convex:** Aggregates veCRV to provide boosted yields without locking

### Balancer

**BAL emissions:** Distributed to eligible pools
**Boosted pools:** Underlying assets deployed to lending for extra yield
**Aura:** Similar to Convex, aggregates veBAL for boosted returns

### Uniswap v3

No native farm rewards, but:
- Fee APYs can be high for active positions
- Third-party farms sometimes incentivize specific pools
- Merkl distributes some project incentives to v3 LPs

## Risk Management

### Impermanent Loss Awareness

IL erodes farming gains. Calculate expected IL for your pairs and time horizon. Use IL calculators before depositing.

### Smart Contract Diversification

Do not put all capital in one protocol. Even audited contracts can fail.

### Exit Planning

Know how you will exit before entering:
- Withdrawal lockups?
- Exit fees?
- Liquidity for reward tokens?

### Monitoring

Check positions regularly:
- Are rewards still active?
- Has TVL changed significantly?
- Any governance changes affecting yields?

Tools like DeBank, Zapper, and Zerion aggregate positions across protocols.

## Tax Considerations

Yield farming creates complex tax situations:
- LP token minting may be taxable
- Reward claims are typically income
- Compounding creates new cost basis
- Impermanent loss treatment varies by jurisdiction

Keep detailed records. Tax software like Koinly or CoinTracker helps track DeFi transactions.

## Common Mistakes

**Chasing highest APY:** Sky-high yields often crash. Sustainable returns beat volatile ones.

**Ignoring gas:** Frequent compounding on mainnet can consume all profits.

**Overlooking IL:** A 50% APY farm with 30% IL nets only 20%.

**Not reading contracts:** Understand lockups, fees, and withdrawal mechanics.

**Overexposure:** Putting all funds in farming maximizes risk, not returns.

## Summary

Yield farming combines LP fees with token incentives for enhanced returns. Success requires evaluating sustainable yields, managing multiple risks, and maintaining disciplined position management.

Start with established protocols and simple strategies. Add complexity as you gain experience. Remember that high yields always come with high risks.
