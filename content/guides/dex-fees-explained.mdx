## The Three Types of DEX Fees

Every DEX trade involves multiple fees. Understanding each helps you minimize costs and choose optimal trading venues.

### 1. Swap Fees (Protocol Fees)

The DEX protocol charges a percentage of each trade. This fee goes to liquidity providers and sometimes the protocol treasury.

| DEX | Standard Fee |
|-----|--------------|
| Uniswap v2 | 0.30% |
| Uniswap v3 | 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.30%, or 1.00% |
| SushiSwap | 0.30% (0.25% to LPs, 0.05% to treasury) |
| Curve | 0.04% for stablecoin pools |
| PancakeSwap | 0.25% |

A $1,000 swap on Uniswap v2 costs $3 in swap fees. On Curve's stablecoin pools, the same swap costs $0.40.

### 2. Network Fees (Gas)

Blockchain transactions require gas, paid to validators who process the transaction. Gas varies by:
- Network congestion
- Transaction complexity
- Chain used

Typical gas costs for a simple swap:
- Ethereum mainnet: $5-50
- Arbitrum: $0.10-1.00
- Base: $0.05-0.50
- Solana: $0.001-0.01
- BNB Chain: $0.05-0.30

Gas is paid regardless of swap size. A $100 swap and a $100,000 swap cost similar gas.

### 3. Price Impact

Large trades relative to pool liquidity receive worse prices. This is not a fee collected by anyone, but a cost to you.

Example: ETH/USDC pool with $10M liquidity
- $1,000 swap: 0.01% price impact
- $100,000 swap: 1% price impact
- $500,000 swap: 5% price impact

Price impact increases quadratically with trade size. Splitting large trades often reduces total cost.

## Calculating Total Trading Cost

**Total cost = Swap fee + Gas + Price impact**

### Example 1: Small Trade on Ethereum

Swapping $200 ETH to USDC on Uniswap v3 (0.30% pool):
- Swap fee: $200 * 0.30% = $0.60
- Gas: $15 (congested period)
- Price impact: negligible
- **Total: $15.60 (7.8% of trade value)**

### Example 2: Small Trade on Solana

Swapping $200 SOL to USDC on Jupiter:
- Swap fee: $200 * 0.30% = $0.60
- Gas: $0.005
- Price impact: negligible
- **Total: $0.605 (0.3% of trade value)**

### Example 3: Large Trade on Arbitrum

Swapping $50,000 ETH to USDC on Uniswap v3 (0.05% pool):
- Swap fee: $50,000 * 0.05% = $25
- Gas: $0.50
- Price impact: 0.5% = $250
- **Total: $275.50 (0.55% of trade value)**

## Minimizing Costs

### Choose the Right Chain

For trades under $1,000, gas dominates. Use low-fee chains:
- Solana for near-zero gas
- Base, Arbitrum, Optimism for sub-dollar gas
- BNB Chain for cheap EVM transactions

For trades over $10,000, swap fees and price impact matter more. Ethereum mainnet may be acceptable if it offers better liquidity.

### Use Appropriate Fee Tiers

Uniswap v3 offers multiple fee tiers:
- 0.01%: Stablecoins (USDC/USDT)
- 0.05%: Correlated pairs (ETH/stETH)
- 0.30%: Standard pairs (ETH/USDC)
- 1.00%: Exotic pairs

Most volume concentrates in 0.05% pools for major pairs. Check which tier has the best liquidity for your trade.

### Aggregate for Large Trades

DEX aggregators (1inch, Jupiter, Paraswap) split trades across multiple pools and DEXs to minimize price impact. A $100,000 trade might route through 5 different pools.

### Time Your Trades

Gas prices follow predictable patterns. Ethereum is cheapest during off-peak hours (nights and weekends in US). Track gas at etherscan.io/gastracker.

### Set Appropriate Slippage

High slippage tolerance (5-10%) makes you vulnerable to sandwich attacks. Keep slippage as low as possible for your trade:
- Stablecoins: 0.1%
- Major pairs: 0.5%
- Volatile tokens: 1-3%

## Understanding LP Fee Earnings

If you provide liquidity, you earn a portion of swap fees.

### Fee Distribution

In a $10M pool with 0.30% fees and $5M daily volume:
- Daily fees: $5,000,000 * 0.30% = $15,000
- If you provide $100,000 (1% of pool): $150/day
- Annualized: approximately 55% APY (before impermanent loss)

### Fee Tier Selection for LPs

Higher fee tiers earn more per swap but attract less volume. For stable pairs, the 0.01% pool often generates more total fees due to massive volume.

Consider:
- Higher fees = more earnings per trade
- Lower fees = more trade volume
- Optimal depends on the specific pair and market conditions

## Hidden Costs

### MEV and Sandwich Attacks

Bots monitor pending transactions and can extract value:
1. Bot sees your swap order in mempool
2. Bot front-runs with a buy, moving price up
3. Your swap executes at a worse price
4. Bot sells for profit

This costs traders an estimated 0.1-0.5% on vulnerable transactions. Protect yourself:
- Use private RPCs (Flashbots Protect, MEV Blocker)
- Trade on chains with MEV protection (Solana has some built-in)
- Keep slippage tight to limit extraction

### Failed Transactions

On Ethereum, failed transactions still cost gas. Common causes:
- Slippage exceeded during confirmation
- Token transfer restrictions
- Smart contract errors

A failed swap might cost $10-30 in wasted gas.

### Approval Transactions

First-time swaps require approval (one per token per DEX). Approval costs gas without executing any trade. Budget for this when starting.

## Summary

DEX fees include swap fees, gas, and price impact. Small trades are dominated by gas costs (favor low-fee chains). Large trades are dominated by swap fees and price impact (favor deep liquidity and aggregators).

Understanding the fee structure helps you choose when, where, and how to trade. A 5-minute analysis before large trades can save hundreds of dollars.
