## Problem

With 50+ DEX reviews planned, editorial voice must be consistent. Without a style guide, content may vary in tone, terminology, and quality. Inconsistency hurts brand trust.

## Solution

Create style guide covering:
- Tone: Informative, direct, no hype
- Terminology: "DEX" not "dex", "TVL" defined on first use
- Forbidden phrases: "in the ever-evolving world of...", "it's important to note..."
- Structure: How to write Editor's Take sections
- Length guidelines: Min/max for each content type
- Attribution: "DexRank Editorial Team" only

Store in `/content/STYLE_GUIDE.md` or similar.
