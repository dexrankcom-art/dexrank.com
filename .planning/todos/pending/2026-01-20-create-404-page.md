## Problem

Default 404 page provides no help to users who hit dead links. With 1500+ DEX pages and future URL changes, 404s will happen. A helpful 404 reduces bounce rate and keeps users on site.

## Solution

Create custom 404 page (`app/not-found.tsx`) with:
- Clear "Page not found" message
- Search box to find DEXs
- Links to popular pages (homepage, top DEXs, guides)
- Suggestion: "Looking for a DEX? Try searching below"

Keep it simple and helpful, not clever.
