## Problem

DexRank monetizes via affiliate partnerships (Hyperliquid, GMX, dYdX, etc.). FTC requires clear disclosure of affiliate relationships. Missing disclosures = legal risk and trust issues.

## Solution

Add affiliate disclosure:
1. Global footer disclaimer: "DexRank may earn commission from partner links"
2. Per-page disclosure near CTAs: "This link is an affiliate link"
3. Dedicated disclosure page: `/affiliate-disclosure` explaining relationships

Keep disclosures clear but not intrusive. Standard practice for comparison sites.
