## Problem

Phase 3 (Content & Differentiation) will involve generating articles and content for DEX pages. Risk of content feeling generic, repetitive, or obviously AI-generated ("AI slop"). Need strategy to ensure generated content is:
- Factually accurate and data-driven
- Unique per DEX (not template-fill)
- Valuable to users (not just SEO filler)
- Written in authentic voice

## Solution

Potential approaches (TBD - needs research):
- Use actual protocol data (TVL, volume, chains) to drive content uniqueness
- Include specific on-chain metrics that change per protocol
- Avoid generic filler phrases and boilerplate
- Human review workflow before publishing
- Pull real data points that make each article genuinely different
- Consider hybrid approach: AI drafts + human editing
- Template constraints that force specificity
- Quality scoring/filtering before publish
